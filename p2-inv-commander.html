Audit: Military Commander

Under IHL, military commanders bear the primary responsibility for ensuring civilian protection and minimizing collateral damage during kinetic targeting. The commander in this scenario deliberately set the AI confidence threshold to 90%, arguably defining the parameters of acceptable risk.

However, the commander did not personally select the target nor authorize the final strike in the moment. The integration of AI introduces "distributed agency" between human hierarchies and machines. The commander relied on the testing and certification of the AI developers, believing 90% to be a legally compliant threshold. Because they were far removed from the actual execution of the strike, prosecuting the commander for a direct war crime becomes legally ambiguous.
Sources: (Boulanin et al., 2024), (Geiß, 2019)
