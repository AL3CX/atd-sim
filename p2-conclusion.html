The Accountability Gap

As your investigation reveals, the introduction of AI into kinetic targeting creates a systemic "Accountability Gap." The commander sets the rules but does not pull the trigger. The operator pulls the trigger but is robbed of meaningful cognitive control by automation bias and extreme time limits. The machine cannot be put on trial, and the developers are distant civilian contractors. No one is fully to blame, yet a civilian is dead.

This simulation is a fictionalized abstraction, but the ethical failure it represents is very real. It is created in remembrance of Abdul-Rahman al-Rawi and others who have suffered as a result of algorithmic warfare. While the exact operational details of Military X are generalized, Abdul-Rahman al-Rawi represents the first confirmed civilian killed in an AI-assisted military strike.
