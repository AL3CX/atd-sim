Audit: AI System & Developers

The AI targeting algorithm ultimately generated the "kill list" and assigned the 90%+ confidence score that led to the strike. Many argue the developers or the machine itself should bear the blame for incorrectly identifying a civilian as a valid target. The ethics of AI dictates that algorithmic systems carry inherent biases from their training data.

However, software engineers are far removed from the theater of war and are constructing decision-support tools, not autonomous agents legally capable of committing war crimes. Furthermore, advanced machine learning operates as a "black box," making it technically impossible in many cases to ascertain exactly why the AI generated a specific target. Neither lines of code nor remote civilian programmers can realistically be prosecuted under current IHL frameworks.
Sources: (Stahl et al., 2022), (Geiß, 2019)
