AI-Assisted Targeting Dilemma Simulator (ATD-SIM)

As AI technologies advance, they increasingly become more integrated into military applications. Specifically AI is being integrated into decision-making processes due to their capabilities to process large amounts of data at high speeds. However, states are obligated to comply with international humanitarian law (IHL) and the ethical risks- which will be discussed throughout the simulation- of using AI in military applications are growing significantly more complex as AI technologies advances.

PURPOSE

The ATD Simulator is designed to help users understand the ethical and legal implications of using AI in military applications, specifically in the context of selecting targets which is actively being used today.
