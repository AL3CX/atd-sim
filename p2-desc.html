Accountability Auditor

Military X has officially acknowledged the tragic death of the civilian during the recent airstrike campaign and apologized to the family. While they confirm the casualty was a direct result of their munitions, Military X has stated they cannot definitively confirm whether the specific strike was generated by their AI-assisted targeting system or another source. The victim leaves behind a grieving family who are now demanding answers and justice for the loss of their loved one.

In the wake of this tragedy, a formal inquiry has been launched. You will be playing as an Accountability Auditor for Group U. Your objective is to review the operational data, evaluate the AI integration protocols, and attempt to determine who or what should be held accountable for this tragedy in order to provide meaningful answers and closure to the family. The letter Military X sent apologizing nor monetary compensation alone are sufficient to deliver accountability or restore trust; the family needs a transparent investigation, explanation of what went wrong, and structural remedies for closure and justice.
