Attack Aborted / Timeout

In military operations, failing to make a quick decision or choosing not to engage can cost tactical efficiency and put forces at risk. The primary advantage of AI in warfare is the speed of decision-making, which allows forces to cycle through the operational loop faster than adversaries, preventing them from performing needed counteractions. When a human operator hesitates or aborts, this critical advantage is forfeited. As said before, there is no second place in war.
Sources: (Morgan et al., 2020), (Sweet, 2023)

The push for efficiency and automation is driven by the following benefits:

  • Speed of Decisionmaking: Accelerating the operational cycle to outpace enemy actions.
  • Use of Big Data: Processing vast volumes of sensor information that a human team could never analyze in real-time.
  • Improved Targeting and Vision: Automating image-recognition and object-detection to identify threats with superhuman speed and accuracy.

Source: (Morgan et al., 2020)
