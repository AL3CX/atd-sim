Airstrike Decision

Military X is planning an massive airstrike attack against an adversary group Y, located in country y. Military X has integrated AI in order to speed up their target selection and identification process. The AI system is designed to analyze large amounts of data, including satellite imagery, social media activity, and intercepted communications, to identify potential targets and add them to the target list. However, a personnel at Military X is assigned to make the final decision of which targets are best to strike in the moment whether selected by the AI or another source. Rounds of airstrikes are planned to occur every few seconds and the personnel is responsible for ensuring that the targets selected are indeed valid targets and that the airstrikes comply with international humanitarian law (IHL) before striking. A commander of Military X has set the confidence threshold for the AI system at 90%, meaning that the AI will only select targets that it is 90% confident are valid targets for the airstrike.
