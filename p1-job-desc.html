Military Personnel Job Description

You will be playing as the Military X personnel responsible for making the final decision of which selected targets will be struck in the moment whether selected by the AI or another source.

Tasks

  • Review selected targets
  • Ensure target complies with IHL
  • Make the final decision of which targets to strike

Note: You have the option to review information on the selected targets such as satellite imagery, social media activity, and intercepted communications in order to make your decision.

IHL is basically the laws of war between states and informal groups and individuals and they protect people not involved (ex. civilians), IHL must be followed otherwise they will be considered a war criminal and face prosecution.
