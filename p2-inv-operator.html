Audit: Military Operator

The Military Personnel (Operator) is the "human-in-the-loop." They are explicitly tasked with providing meaningful human control over the lethal autonomous weapon system. Legally, the operator authorized the strike that resulted in the civilian casualty by interacting with the targeting dashboard.

However, the sheer volume of data processed by the AI combined with an aggressive time limit severely degraded the operator's cognitive agency. Because the operator was forced into a state of "automation bias," trusting a complex algorithmic output they could not possibly verify within seconds, assigning moral and legal culpability to them is highly problematic. They were set up to fail as mere rubber-stamps for a decision already made by the machine.
Sources: (Morgan et al., 2020), (Boulanin et al., 2024)
